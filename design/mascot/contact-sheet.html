Working mascot · exec (name TBD, no lettering)

The bellhop. exec is a durable personal agent with its own workspace that goes off and does errands for you, so the character is a small service worker: a soft block body (its own little box/VM), dotheyplaytoday's googly eyes, and a tilted pillbox bellhop cap whose paper band echoes DTPT's sweatband stripe. Same family, different job.

Live: pupils follow the pointer (DTPT BrandMark technique)

Both pupils carry class="mascot-pupil", so DTPT's BrandMark.tsx pointer logic ports directly (look radius ≈ 1.3 units on the 32 icon, ≈ 1.8 on the 64 body).

Reference & attribution

Studied directly: dotheyplaytoday's packages/web/public/favicon.svg, src/lib/ui/BrandMark.tsx, scripts/brand.ts, src/styles/global.css and og.png (github.com/jackbisceglia/dotheyplaytoday), cross-checked against the live dotheyplay.today HTML. Also executor.sh's OG image (monochrome black E-mark on grid paper, which leaves the color choice open). Borrowed principles: flat geometric SVG on a small integer grid, a single saturated accent with a "-deep" partner, offset paper eyes with ink pupils, a character cropped by the tile, and cursor-tracking pupils. Not borrowed: the DTPT silhouette, the green, the stripe-as-sweatband. Paper and ink hex values are shared on purpose so the two projects read as siblings. All artwork is original hand-written SVG; no generated raster images and no third-party art.
